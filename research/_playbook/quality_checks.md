# Quality Checks

Validation steps to ensure research quality.

## Mandatory Checks

### 1. Critic Sub-Agent Review
After completing initial research, spawn a sub-agent with this mandate:
- Challenge the main conclusions
- Identify logical gaps or unsupported claims
- Find counterarguments or alternative interpretations
- Rate confidence in findings (high/medium/low)

### 2. Source Verification
- [ ] Are sources cited and accessible?
- [ ] Are sources credible (peer-reviewed, expert, primary)?
- [ ] Are multiple independent sources used?
- [ ] Are sources recent enough for the topic?

### 3. Bias Check
- [ ] Did I seek out opposing viewpoints?
- [ ] Am I over-relying on one source or perspective?
- [ ] Am I confirming pre-existing beliefs?
- [ ] Are there stakeholders whose views I missed?

### 4. Comprehension Test
- [ ] Can I explain this to a beginner?
- [ ] Can I answer "why" questions, not just "what"?
- [ ] Do I understand the limitations of this knowledge?

## Optional Checks (for high-stakes research)

### Expert Validation
- Identify domain experts
- Cross-check conclusions against expert consensus
- Note where findings diverge from mainstream

### Reproducibility
- Can someone else follow my sources and reach similar conclusions?
- Are my reasoning steps documented?

## Red Flags

Watch for these warning signs:
- Only finding confirming evidence (too clean)
- Single source dominance
- Circular citations (sources citing each other)
- Vague or unverifiable claims
- Emotional language in sources (may indicate bias)
