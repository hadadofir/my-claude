# Anti-Patterns

Research mistakes to avoid. Updated as lessons are learned.

## Discovery Phase

### Premature Depth
**Problem**: Diving deep into the first source before mapping the landscape.
**Fix**: Do a broad survey first, then prioritize where to go deep.

### Echo Chamber
**Problem**: Only reading sources that agree with initial hypothesis.
**Fix**: Explicitly search for counterarguments and critics.

### Authority Worship
**Problem**: Accepting claims because of who said them.
**Fix**: Evaluate arguments on their merits; experts can be wrong.

## Analysis Phase

### Confirmation Bias
**Problem**: Interpreting ambiguous evidence as supporting your view.
**Fix**: Use critic sub-agent; ask "what would change my mind?"

### False Precision
**Problem**: Presenting uncertain findings as definitive.
**Fix**: Use confidence levels; distinguish "likely" from "proven."

### Complexity Theater
**Problem**: Making explanations complex to seem thorough.
**Fix**: Simplicity is a sign of understanding, not weakness.

## Output Phase

### Kitchen Sink
**Problem**: Including everything found, regardless of relevance.
**Fix**: Ruthlessly prioritize; what does the learner actually need?

### Missing Context
**Problem**: Presenting conclusions without showing reasoning.
**Fix**: Show the path from evidence to conclusion.
